# Bearing Inspection & Replacement Procedure
## Document: MX-PROC-001 | UAV Turbine Engine Maintenance Manual

### 1. Purpose
This procedure covers inspection, assessment, and replacement of main shaft bearings
in small turbine/jet engines (JetCat P-series and equivalent).

### 2. When to Perform
- Vibration readings exceed 2.5g sustained for more than 10 seconds
- Oil temperature exceeds 110°C during normal cruise
- Oil pressure drops below 40 PSI
- Scheduled maintenance at 200 flight-hour intervals
- Digital twin health factor drops below 0.85

### 3. Required Tools & Parts
- Bearing puller set (SKF TMMA series or equivalent)
- Dial indicator with magnetic base
- Replacement bearings (ceramic hybrid recommended)
- High-temperature grease (Kluber Isoflex NBU 15)
- Torque wrench (5-50 Nm range)
- Bore gauge

### 4. Safety Precautions
- Ensure engine is completely cool (below 50°C)
- Disconnect fuel supply and electrical connections
- Lock out all controls — apply "ENGINE MAINTENANCE" placard
- Wear heat-resistant gloves and eye protection

### 5. Inspection Procedure
1. Remove engine cowling and access panels
2. Rotate shaft by hand — feel for roughness, catching, or play
3. Mount dial indicator on bearing housing
4. Measure radial play: MAX allowable = 0.05mm
5. Measure axial play: MAX allowable = 0.08mm
6. Inspect bearing races for pitting, spalling, or discoloration
7. Check oil for metallic particles (magnetic plug inspection)

### 6. Replacement Procedure
1. Document bearing part numbers and orientation
2. Remove retaining hardware using correct torque sequence (reverse)
3. Heat bearing housing to 80°C for thermal expansion (DO NOT use open flame)
4. Extract bearing using puller — do NOT strike with hammer
5. Clean bearing seat with approved solvent
6. Inspect shaft for scoring or wear
7. Install new bearing — cool bearing in freezer for 30 min before press fit
8. Apply specified grease quantity (do NOT over-grease)
9. Torque retaining hardware to specification
10. Rotate shaft — verify smooth, free rotation

### 7. Post-Replacement Checks
- Run engine at idle for 5 minutes
- Monitor vibration: must be below 1.5g at idle
- Monitor oil temperature: must stabilize below 95°C
- Monitor oil pressure: must be above 45 PSI
- Perform test at 50%, 75%, 100% throttle — 30 seconds each
- Record all readings in maintenance log

### 8. Estimated Downtime
- Inspection only: 2-3 hours
- Bearing replacement: 6-8 hours
- Full overhaul with shaft inspection: 12-16 hours
