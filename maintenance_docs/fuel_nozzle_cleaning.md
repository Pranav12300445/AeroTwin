# Fuel Nozzle Cleaning & Inspection Procedure
## Document: MX-PROC-003 | UAV Turbine Engine Fuel System Maintenance

### 1. Purpose
Procedure for cleaning, inspecting, and replacing fuel nozzles in small turbine engines.
Fuel nozzle clogging is a common cause of combustion inefficiency and EGT imbalance.

### 2. Symptoms of Fuel Nozzle Clogging
- Gradual decrease in fuel flow rate below normal range
- EGT fluctuations or asymmetry between expected and actual values
- Erratic EGT readings (±40°C swings)
- Gradual RPM decrease despite constant throttle
- Digital twin residual trending upward on EGT channel
- Increased fuel consumption for given thrust level

### 3. Inspection Schedule
- **Routine**: Every 100 flight hours
- **Condition-based**: When fuel flow drops >15% from baseline
- **Post-event**: After any fuel contamination incident

### 4. Required Tools & Materials
- Ultrasonic cleaning bath
- Approved solvent (MIL-PRF-680 Type II or equivalent)
- Flow bench with calibrated flow meters
- Magnification loupe (10x minimum)
- Replacement nozzle O-rings and seals
- Fuel filter elements
- Compressed air (dry, oil-free)

### 5. Removal Procedure
1. Drain fuel system — capture fuel in approved container
2. Disconnect fuel supply lines at nozzle manifold
3. Remove nozzle retaining hardware
4. Extract nozzles — note position numbering
5. Cap all open fuel lines to prevent contamination
6. Inspect nozzle seats for corrosion or damage

### 6. Cleaning Procedure
1. Soak nozzles in approved solvent for 30 minutes
2. Place in ultrasonic bath at 40°C for 15 minutes
3. Flush each nozzle orifice with solvent using syringe
4. Blow dry with compressed air
5. Inspect orifice under magnification:
   - Orifice diameter must be within ±0.02mm of specification
   - No carbon deposits visible
   - No erosion or enlargement of spray holes
6. If deposits remain after cleaning — REPLACE nozzle

### 7. Flow Testing
1. Mount nozzle on flow bench
2. Test at calibrated pressure (manufacturer spec)
3. Measure flow rate — must be within ±5% of rated flow
4. Inspect spray pattern:
   - Must be symmetrical cone pattern
   - No streaking or dripping
   - Atomization must be fine and uniform
5. Record all measurements

### 8. Installation Procedure
1. Replace all O-rings and seals (mandatory)
2. Install nozzles in correct positions
3. Torque to specification
4. Reconnect fuel lines
5. Perform fuel system leak check at operating pressure
6. Engine ground run:
   - Idle for 5 minutes — check for leaks
   - 50% throttle — verify fuel flow in normal range
   - 100% throttle — verify EGT within limits
   - Monitor digital twin residual — should return to baseline

### 9. Estimated Downtime
- Cleaning and flow test: 3-4 hours
- Nozzle replacement: 4-6 hours
- Full fuel system overhaul: 8-12 hours
