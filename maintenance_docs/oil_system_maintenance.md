# Oil System Maintenance & Troubleshooting
## Document: MX-PROC-005 | UAV Turbine Engine Lubrication System

### 1. Purpose
Maintenance procedures for the engine oil system, covering oil changes,
filter inspection, and troubleshooting of oil pressure and temperature anomalies.

### 2. Oil System Overview
The lubrication system provides oil to:
- Main shaft bearings (front and rear)
- Accessory gearbox bearings
- Fuel pump drive bearings

Oil flow path: Tank → Pump → Filter → Bearings → Scavenge → Cooler → Tank

### 3. Normal Operating Parameters
| Parameter | Normal | Warning | Action Required |
|-----------|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Oil Pressure | 45-65 PSI | <40 PSI | Land ASAP, inspect |
| Oil Temperature | 70-95°C | >105°C | Reduce power, monitor |
| Oil Consumption | <5 mL/hour | >10 mL/hour | Inspect seals |

### 4. Routine Oil Change (Every 50 hours)
1. Run engine to operating temperature (warm oil drains better)
2. Shut down and wait 5 minutes (allow oil to settle)
3. Place drain container under oil tank drain
4. Remove drain plug — allow complete drainage (10 minutes)
5. Inspect drain plug magnetic chip detector:
   - Clean = normal
   - Fine powder = monitor, acceptable
   - Metal flakes or chunks = GROUND ENGINE, investigate
6. Replace oil filter element
7. Inspect old filter for debris
8. Install new drain plug gasket
9. Fill with approved oil to MAX mark
10. Ground run — verify pressure and temperature

### 5. Troubleshooting: Low Oil Pressure
| Possible Cause | Diagnostic | Corrective Action |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| Low oil level | Check sight glass | Add oil, check for leaks |
| Clogged filter | Check differential pressure | Replace filter |
| Worn oil pump | Bench test pump output | Replace pump |
| Bearing wear | Check vibration data | Bearing inspection (MX-PROC-001) |
| Oil cooler blockage | Check cooler inlet/outlet temp | Flush or replace cooler |

### 6. Troubleshooting: High Oil Temperature
| Possible Cause | Diagnostic | Corrective Action |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| Bearing degradation | Correlate with vibration | Bearing inspection |
| Oil cooler failure | Check cooler airflow | Clean or replace cooler |
| Wrong oil grade | Verify oil specification | Drain and refill correct grade |
| Excessive engine load | Review flight profile | Adjust operating parameters |
| Oil breakdown | Oil sample analysis | Oil change + investigation |

### 7. Oil Sample Analysis Guide
Send oil sample to approved lab. Key indicators:
- **Iron (Fe)**: >50 ppm = bearing or gear wear
- **Copper (Cu)**: >30 ppm = bearing cage wear
- **Silicon (Si)**: >25 ppm = dirt ingestion
- **Aluminum (Al)**: >20 ppm = piston or housing wear
- **Viscosity change**: >10% = oil degradation

### 8. Estimated Downtime
- Routine oil change: 1-2 hours
- Oil system troubleshooting: 2-4 hours
- Oil pump replacement: 4-6 hours
- Complete oil system overhaul: 8-12 hours
